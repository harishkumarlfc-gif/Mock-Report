00 / Executive Summary

Seven events. Two cities. 298 founders. Aurelia Global present at every one.

September started in Bengaluru with a growth-stage founders dinner on the 9th and a Founders Walk on the 12th. The series returned to Bengaluru on 1st November with the Tech Founders Walk. 116 founders across all three Bengaluru events, 37 of them running companies above $500K ARR.

San Francisco followed. Four events across six weeks, part of the AI Bhoomi Caravan, SF Tech Week, and TechCrunch Disrupt: a founders dinner on 22nd September, a breakfast and breakthrough session on the 25th, a founders hike on the 27th that drew 76 people and ran well past schedule, and a second founders hike on 10th October. 182 founders in total across the SF leg.

Across September, October, and November: 56 MQLs in the pipeline. 10 SQL conversations opened. November has just begun, and we are just getting started.
