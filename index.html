VISIBILITY & REPRESENTATION

Evangelism

Aurelia Global was front and center across all seven events—three in Bengaluru across a Founders Dinner and Founders Walks, and four across San Francisco & London spanning the AI Bhoomi Caravan, SF Tech Week, and TechCrunch Disrupt.

TAKING AURELIA TO SAN FRANCISCO

We took Aurelia Global to San Francisco across four events: a founders dinner, a breakfast and breakthrough session, and two Sawyer Camp founders hikes, embedded within the AI Bhoomi Caravan, SF Tech Week, and TechCrunch Disrupt—spaces where all the major cross-border fintechs have their presence. Aurelia Global was also represented at the VSAS Collective and Vertex, extending brand reach into additional rooms with India–US corridor founders and operators.

HYDERABAD & BENGALURU PARTNER ACTIVATION

GTM Unbound introduced the Aurelia Global team to Commenda and FinStackk in Hyderabad and Bengaluru. Both have strong standing in the local startup community and existing relationships with the exact founder profile Aurelia Global is looking to reach. Warm introductions in their networks have already opened active pipeline conversations.

COMPETITOR WATCH: AIRWALLEX & STRIPE

Three moves worth flagging from the India–US corridor space. Skydo is now partnering with corridor communities directly, running the same rooms and networks Aurelia Global is working to occupy. But the bigger signal this cycle is Airwallex—and it is not a hiring move.

Airwallex has placed a structural bet on San Francisco. They became the SF Giants’ Official Jersey Patch Partner and Official Financial Software Partner—a multi-year deal that puts their name stitched into every game at Oracle Park, paired with private suite nights for growth-stage founders and committing $1B+ to US operations through 2029.

Stripe is moving in a similar direction—running high-end founder experiences for growth-stage companies in the corridor. The pattern across all three is the same: fintech infrastructure companies have concluded that the highest-leverage distribution play in this market is community proximity, not advertising.

A BIGGER IDEA: CREATING EXPERIENCES THAT BUILD LASTING BRAND CONNECTIONS

One idea we’d love to explore with Aurelia Global is creating exclusive, high-memorability experiences for founders who fit your ICP—from watching a cricket match together in a private hospitality box to an intimate chef’s table evening. When founders look back on a memorable experience, they remember Aurelia Global as the brand that brought their peer group together.
